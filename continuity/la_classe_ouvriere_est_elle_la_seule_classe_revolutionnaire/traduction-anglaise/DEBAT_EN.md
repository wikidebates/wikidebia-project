# Is the working class the only revolutionary class?

Working class · Debate under construction


## Arguments for

- The working class is the only social force capable of overthrowing capitalism
- The working class is the only class that can bring about a new economic order free from exploitation
- The working class is the only class capable of uniting and organising for revolution
- The working class is the only class capable of seeing the revolutionary struggle through to the end
- The working class has demonstrated its ability to carry out a revolution
- The other classes are not revolutionary

## Arguments against

- The working class does not exist
- The concept of the working class is problematic
- The working class is too integrated into bourgeois society
- The working class has lost its economic power
- Working-class organisations have lost their base and legitimacy
- The working class has lost its militancy
- The working class is too divided to be conscious of itself as a class
- A revolution can and must take place without the working class
- We must give up the fantasy of revolution
